From frontier to owned, one task at a time.

02 · the path
01

Collect

Your agents keep running on the frontier model. Every call, tool call and outcome is captured and redacted in your cloud.

CC · gateway connector
02

Verify

Library and custom checks decide which answers were actually right. Only those become golden examples.

CT · verifiers
03

Train

An open model learns the task from the golden set, rewarded by the same checks.

CT · RL on your compute
04

Hand off

Once it matches the frontier on that task, traffic moves over. If it slips, the task moves back.

Is your task a good fit?

03 · fit check

A good fit

  • ✓
    High volumeHundreds or thousands of runs a week, so there's plenty to learn from.
  • ✓
    An outcome you can checkA ledger entry, a passing test, a number that matches.
  • ✓
    Stable rulesPolicies change, but not every day.
  • ✓
    A safe fallbackWhen the model isn't sure, the frontier or a person takes it.

Not yet

  • –
    Open-ended creative workNo single right answer to check against.
  • –
    No ground truthNothing in your systems confirms the outcome.
  • –
    Rare, one-off tasksToo few examples to learn from, for now.
